This study treats multiple landslide inventories as imperfect observers of a shared event, separating landslide occurrence from the process that records it. Across simulated reporting bias and real inventories, the approach reduces road bias and improves discrimination in several settings while identifying cases where standard neutralisation remains as accurate.
